# DESIGN.md — candidate direction: Frame.io ("midnight projection room")

Source: refero-styles MCP, `refero_design_md("frame.io")`, style id 30c3aa18-4323-4448-8ddd-3ca933fe5780
(https://styles.refero.design/styles/30c3aa18-4323-4448-8ddd-3ca933fe5780). Found via
`refero_search("video streaming tv cinema dark")`; `refero_similar` on it returned Vimeo, Framer,
Stink Studios, Design Full-Time (all dark "cinema" systems).

Why this one: Cast Bridge is used in a dim room in front of a TV. The current light neumorphic
kit is bright, low-contrast and puts soft shadows on every control. A dark canvas with one
accent keeps the video/thumbnail as the only bright thing on screen, which is what every
player reference (Hulu, Infuse, Matter) does.

How it maps to Cast Bridge (research note, not a build spec):
- Obsidian #0a0a13 = app canvas; Graphite #08080c = dock and sheets; Charcoal #2a2a32 = hairlines.
- Iris Glow #6199f6 = the ONLY accent: "casting live" state, detected-media badge, active tab.
- Pills (100px radius) for the device chip and dock buttons; no drop shadows on controls.
- Marketing-site rules below (1280px width, gradient backgrounds, 48–80px display type) do
  not transfer to a phone app shell; take the palette, restraint and pill geometry only.
- Colours are the source site's. If Rj supplies his own, his values win.

## Do

- 100px border-radius for all buttons, nav items and pills
- Display headlines at weight 400 with tight negative letter-spacing; never bold for impact
- Reserve Iris Glow (#6199f6) for icons, labels, links and active states
- Uppercase 12px eyebrow label (0.06em tracking) as a section marker
- Layer at least one gradient on page backgrounds (marketing pages)
- Max content width 1280px, backgrounds full-bleed (marketing pages)
- Body/feature text 14–18px in #a3a3b3 or #757580
- Product imagery in 10px-radius containers with a 1px #4f4f80 border

## Don't

- No weight 600/700 display headlines
- No additional accent colours
- No flat #000000 full-page background
- No card backgrounds or heavy borders on feature columns
- No serif or decorative typefaces
- No drop shadows on buttons or nav items
- No centred body paragraphs; left-align, ~560px max width
- Eyebrow label style only for section identifiers
