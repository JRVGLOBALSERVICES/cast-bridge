Cast Bridge for partners

Let your viewers put your videos on their TV

Add one script tag to your site. Every video on the page gets a Send to Cast Bridge button. Viewers tap it, and Cast Bridge sends the video to their TV by Chromecast, AirPlay or the TV's own browser.

Add it to your page

<script src="https://cast.jrvsystems.app/assets/embed.js" async></script>

Put it anywhere in your HTML. Players that add their video after the page loads are picked up too.

Try it

This page loads the same script. The button below the video is what your viewers see.

What your viewers do

  1. Tap Send to Cast Bridge under the video.
  2. Cast Bridge opens with the video ready to play.
  3. They tap Cast to TV, AirPlay or Play it on the TV. The video moves to the TV, and the phone works as the remote.

Options

Add thisWhat it does
data-cast-src="…" on the videoThe address to send. You need this if your player uses hls.js, Video.js or Shaka, because those play from a blob: address that only works in that tab. Give the real .m3u8 or .mp4.
data-cast-title="…"The title shown on the TV. Without it, the page title is used.
data-cast-bridge="off"No button on this video.
data-auto="false" on the scriptOnly videos you mark with data-cast-bridge get a button.
data-label="Watch on TV" on the scriptChanges the button text.
data-cast-bridge-buttonTurns your own button or link into the send button. Put the address in data-cast-src or href.

From JavaScript

CastBridge.send('https://media.example.com/service/master.m3u8', {
  title: 'Sunday service'
});

Call CastBridge.scan() if you swap videos in a way the page cannot see.

What your videos need

  • A link that plays without signing in. The TV fetches the video itself and has none of your viewer's cookies. For members-only videos, put a signed link in data-cast-src that stays valid for the length of the video.
  • No DRM. Encrypted video shows a black screen on the TV, so the button hides itself when a video is encrypted.
  • MP4, WebM or HLS work best. If your server doesn't allow cross-origin requests, Cast Bridge relays the stream so the TV can still play it. It doesn't get past sign-in or hotlink checks. That stays under your control.
  • Only your own or licensed content. Only add the button to videos you have the right to share.
