People with access

Which TVs work, and how
Chromecast, Google TV, Android TV (Sony, TCL, Hisense)
Use Cast to TV. Chrome on Android or desktop Chrome only — other browsers have no Cast API.
Apple TV, AirPlay speakers
Use AirPlay in Safari on iPhone, iPad or Mac.
Samsung and LG
Browsers can't reach these directly. Tap Open in VLC and use VLC's cast icon — it finds them over DLNA. Phone mirroring (Smart View) also works.
Roku, Fire TV
Neither speaks Cast, and no browser can reach them — they need each maker's own app. Open in VLC is the way across: VLC finds them on the Wi‑Fi and this hands it the link.
Mirroring the whole phone screen
Not something a website can do — no browser exposes the screen to a TV. On Android use Chrome's own menu → Cast → Cast screen; on a Samsung, Smart View.
A video already on the phone
The TV fetches over the network and cannot reach the phone's storage, so a file picked here would have nowhere to be fetched from. Send it with VLC, or put it somewhere with a link and paste that.
Subtitles
Paste an .srt or .vtt link under Subtitles. SRT is converted for you — the TV only accepts VTT. Adding one mid-film reloads it and picks up where it was.
A stream that plays here but not on the TV
The TV fetches the link itself, so it has to be a public https address. Links that need a login or a signed cookie won't reach it.
